# Operating Systems: Deadlocks and Resource Allocation

A deadlock occurs when two or more processes block indefinitely because each process holds a resource while waiting to acquire another resource held by another blocked process.

Four necessary Coffman conditions for deadlock:
1. Mutual Exclusion: At least one resource is held in a non-shareable mode.
2. Hold and Wait: A process holds at least one resource and waits for others.
3. No Preemption: Resources cannot be forcibly taken away; they must be released voluntarily.
4. Circular Wait: Processes form a closed chain where each waits for the next.

Banker's Algorithm prevents unsafe allocations by simulating whether a safe execution sequence still exists before granting a request.
