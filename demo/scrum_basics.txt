# Software Engineering: Scrum Backlog and WIP Limits

Scrum organizes software delivery into short iterative sprints backed by a transparent product backlog.

Key Sprint Controls:
- Product Goal: Long-term target guiding backlog prioritization.
- Work In Progress (WIP) Limit: Caps simultaneous tasks (for example, 5 cards max in To do) so team members finish open work before starting new cards.
- Definition of Done: Shared checklist requiring passing automated tests, peer review, and updated documentation before closing a card.
