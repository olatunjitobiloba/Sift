# PHY 121: General Physics II - Semiconductor Devices
## Topic: P-N Junction Diode Conduction

When p-type and n-type semiconductor materials are brought into metallurgical contact, mobile majority charge carriers diffuse across the junction.
Holes diffuse from the p-side to the n-side, while electrons diffuse from the n-side to the p-side.

This leaves uncompensated donor ions in the n-region and acceptor ions in the p-region, creating the depletion region.
An internal electric field is established, creating a barrier potential:
- Approximately 0.7V for Silicon diodes
- Approximately 0.3V for Germanium diodes

Under forward bias condition:
The external positive terminal connects to the p-side and negative terminal to the n-side. This reduces the barrier width and allows large diffusion current to flow through the device.
